Music style

The stylistic signature of his current and recent projects is based on the balance between power and sound research: intense and full bodied voices, six string basses, acoustic and electronic percussions, pianos and synths interweave with the distinct sound of his eight strings electric and twelve strings acoustic guitars. Extreme genres' complex rhythms dialogue with wide melodies and harmonies ranging from classical counterpoint to contemporary modal music. His multifaceted identity is the unavoidable result of the different musical backgrounds and styles he has encounteres during his career, encompassing: funk, rock, ambient, pop, progressive-symphonic metal and modern jazz.

Beginning with local projects

Everything starts in the place Arturo's friends like to call “The office”, a simple but essential space, that was built by Arturo' continuous and passionate investment of all of his time and resources during his university years. This hideaway is the center of his creative life since the biginning of his carreer and gathers all af the leading-edge instruments and equipments of a fully functional rehearsal and recording room hosting late-night jam sessions, technological experimentations, luthiery setups and the writing session that shaped all of Arturo's bands identities from Gli Ufficio to Asphyxia, from Etnapop 2.0 to Metiria and ...

Dawn of national and international success

The turning point comes with Metiria's participation to the Sanremo Rock contest 2025. After being selected for the interregional round, the band's song Sorrow reaches the national grand final (taking place in the world-famous “Teatro Ariston”) and wins the third overall place of the renowned original music competition. This result arouses new interest and medial resonance for the Sicilian formation, which is notified of the possibility to take part to the San Marino Song Contest. Between more than 800 international bands who applied for the three-steps Castings, Metiria's new song Attention seeker makes the top being selected for the semifinals and becoming part of the 40 artists running for Eurovision Song Contest 2026.

A door closes, another one opens

Due to differences of opinion on the unequal commitment between the band's members after three years of incessant work by Arturo, he decides to leave the project he founded and carried. To avoid conflicts related to the registered trademark "Metiria", he still co-owns with the band's singer, it was decided to release the band's first album still featuring him as author, as well as performer of all the guitar parts, but under the new band name of "..."

Present vision & future goals

The journey, built from the ground in a small and independent studio in the heart of Catania, has now grown step by step thanks to constant discipline and research merged with a precise artistic vision: blending creative technique and emotional intensity in a contemporary and recognisable language. This artist's conception leads the current processes concerning the recordings of Metiria'sfirst album and the composition of Arturo's first Solo EP, soon available on all streaming platforms.
