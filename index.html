Music style

The stylistic signature of his current and recent projects is based on the balance between power and sound research: intense and full bodied voices, six string basses, acoustic and electronic percussions, pianos and synths interweave with the distinct sound of his eight strings electric and twelve strings acoustic guitars. Extreme genres' complex rhythms dialogue with wide melodies and harmonies ranging from classical counterpoint to contemporary modal music. His multifaceted identity is the unavoidable result of the different musical backgrounds and styles he has encounteres during his career, encompassing: funk, rock, ambient, pop, progressive-symphonic metal and modern jazz.

Dawn of national and international success

Metiria takes its form and entirety in 2024 in Catania with its debut in the setting of the Benedictine Monastery, but its birth dates back to the first writing sessions held right away in Arturo's small studio. At the beginning of the band's career, only a few stages had been tread: some local contests and festivals, a few live performances in small theaters, and other minor gigs often providing the band with just expense reimbursements. Despite this, the band's vision was already clear and, in on year's time, the first national shows in Basilicata and Calabria have been already put on.

The turning point came with the band's participation to the Sanremo Rock contest. After being selected for the regional phase at first, and for the interregional round subsequently, Metiria's song Sorrow reached the national grand final which took place in the world-famous “Teatro Ariston” winning the third overall place of the renowned original music competition. This result marks the second era of the project arousing new interest and medial resonance for the Sicilian formation. Thanks to this experience the band is notified of the possibility to take part to the San Marino Song Contest. Metiria decides once again, under the wise guidance of guitarist-composer Arturo Caflisch and singer-lyricist Aurora Amas, to catch this opportunity without any hesitation. Between more than 800 bands who applied for the Castings, the band is included in the list of the first 240 gaining the access to the Stage & Live Academy, competing with its new song Attention seeker with numerous national and international artists. The group made the top being selected for the first semifinal, being part of the first 20 of the total of 40 artists running for a place in the grand final.

A door closes, another one opens

Due to differences of opinion on the principles of meritocracy that were supposed to govern the band's progress, after three years of total commitment, sharing his rehearsal and recording room, his live instrumentation, some near-definite arrangements and drafts for many of the mostly awarded songs and transportation means for national and international dates, Arturo Caflisch decides to leave the band he founded and carried up to September 2026. To avoid conflicts related to the registered trademark "Metiria", he still co-owns with the band's frontwoman, it was decided to release the band's first album still featuring him as author, as well as performer of all the guitar parts, but under the new band name of "..."

Present vision & future goals

The journey, built from the ground in a small and independent studio in the heart of Catania, has now grown step by step thanks to constant discipline and research merged with a precise artistic vision: blending creative technique and emotional intensity in a contemporary and recognisable language. This artist's conception leads the current processes concerning the recordings of Metiria'sfirst album and the composition of Arturo's first Solo EP, soon available on all streaming platforms.
