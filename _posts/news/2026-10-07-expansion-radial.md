**METIRIA** was formed in Catania in 2024, bringing together prog, pop and modern metal in a powerful and refined sound where technical precision meets emotional intensity. 
The band features Aurora Amas on vocals, Samuele Mangiaratti on drums, Lorenzo Ragusa on bass, Angelo Li Rosi on keyboards and Arturo Caflisch on guitar. Now, the quintet introduces its first two singles, *Sorrow* and *Attention Seeker*, immediately showcasing a strong, dynamic and energetic identity.

Both singles deliver an organic, well-produced sound, vibrant guitars and an enveloping atmosphere that keeps the tension constantly moving. Aurora Amas also brings bright, powerful vocal nuances that elevate every shift in intensity. 
In *Sorrow*, the lyrics explore fragility, pain and the need for someone to help you escape from yourself, while *Attention Seeker* focuses on a relationship shaped by indifference, the need for attention and the fear of falling without anyone there to catch you. Through it all, Metiria combines expansive melodies and complex rhythms with an emotional weight that keeps you hooked.
