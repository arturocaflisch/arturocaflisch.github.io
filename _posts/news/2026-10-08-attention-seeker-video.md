*Sounds from Italy*

Hailing from Catania, Sicily, modern progressive outfit METIRIA makes a stunning debut with "Attention Seeker," a meticulously crafted 3'11'' track that effortlessly bridges the gap between progressive rock, alternative, and djent-tinged modern metal. Formed in 2024, the five-piece band—featuring Aurora Amas's warm, soulful, and deeply passionate vocals soaring over the intricate interplay of Samuele Mangiaratti's complex rhythms, Lorenzo Ragusa's driving bass, Angelo Li Rosi's textured keyboards, and Arturo Caflisch's edgy guitar work—delivers a sound that is as technically impressive as it is emotionally resonant. The song unfolds with a bittersweet, melodic sensibility and classy arrangements that belie the band's youth, shifting through dynamic contrasts and a strikingly vulnerable emo bridge before settling into a surprisingly chill, contemplative atmosphere. With its lively, organic band interplay and a solid progressive spirit that never sacrifices feeling for flash, "Attention Seeker" is a remarkable first statement from METIRIA, announcing them as a powerful new voice in the European prog scene.
