Music and dances for one night, as a proof of a new future for the Empire subtracted to the clan Pillera-Puntina and now assigned to the Municipality.

One hundred steps, some already taken, many still to be taken, to return to the city a space that has been managed by mafia money until a few years ago. Last night, the Empire nightclub in Catania reopened music and dancing for the first time since the confiscated assets agency assigned it to the municipality for the ARCI and Siciliani Giovani Social Anti-Mafia Festival. 

[...]

Run by a straw man from the Pillera-Puntina clan, it remained open without changing management even after its confiscation in 2016. However, everything stopped in March 2020. From there, the journey of giving it back to the community has its beginning. According to the many associations (Arcigay, the students, ...), this place is a resource that has to gain a high level of attention being a property in perfect condition, which could be reopened in a very short time.
