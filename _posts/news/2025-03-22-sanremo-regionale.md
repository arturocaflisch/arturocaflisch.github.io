METIRIA were born in the heart of Catania at the beginning of 2024. Aurora on vocals, Sam on drums, Giorgia on keyboards, Arturo on guitar, and Lorenzo on bass (also known as the break-down man). 

The desire to experiment with new sounds is written in the bowels of each of them and a prog feeling beats in all of their hearts. For this reason Metiria put soul and seventh in every note. 

“*Sorrow*”, their trademark, is the proof of that, and was built upon the unique touch of their guitarist. This song was performed in the famous Gervasio Auditorium during live performances in Matera achieving great success with the public after being selected among hundreds of other artists. In fact, at this live shows they won the next stop of June 2025 in Palermo at the Teatro Jolly. 

This will be a great opportunity for them to compete against other high-caliber emerging artists, in an ever-compelling musical confrontation. This time it will be the moment of their other unreleased song “*Jura*”, a foreign name that took inspiration from their bassist's strong affinity with Lithuania. 

The ideal firmly believed and carried forward by METIRIA concerns innovative and original music, built from the mix of the very different stylistic backgrounds of their members, making their listeners passionate and embracing what the music market has to offer.
