Music Education

Giorgia Cristina De Pasqualen (born in ... on ...) is an italian pianist and composer whose musical identity combines a solid classical background with a profound interest in rock music, original songwriting and composition for visual media. She began her academic musical education at the Vincenzo Bellini Conservatory in Catania, where she obtained her Bacherlor's degree in Piano in November 2021, graduating with honours (110/110). her graduation programme featured works by Franz Liszt, Maurice Ravel and Claude Debussy, exploring the theme of water through different musical languages and pianistic approaches.

In July 2023, she completed her Master's Degree in Piano at the same istitution, graduating with 110/110 cum laude and a special mention of merit. Her final performance included various compositions by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.

Since 2025, she has been pursuing a Second-Level Postgraduate Diploma in Music Applied to Images at the Conservatory of Rovigo, further developing her interest in film scoring, orchestration and the relationship between music and visual storytelling. Her final performance included various compositions by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.

Style

Angelo Li Rosi possesses a sophisticated harmonic command and the ability to improvise across diverse genres. His creative process is marked by a meticulous — at times exhaustive — focus on structural integrity and the intricate interplay between instruments.
