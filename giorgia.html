Giorgia Cristina De Pasquale

Keys

Siracusa, Italy

Music Education

Giorgia Cristina De Pasquale (born in Siracusa on ...) is an italian pianist and composer whose musical identity combines a solid classical background with a profound interest in rock music, original songwriting and composition for visual media. She began her academic musical education at the Vincenzo Bellini Conservatory in Catania, where she obtained her Bacherlor's degree in Piano in November 2021, graduating with honours (110/110). her graduation programme featured works by Franz Liszt, Maurice Ravel and Claude Debussy, exploring the theme of water through different musical languages and pianistic approaches.

In July 2023, she completed her Master's Degree in Piano at the same istitution, graduating with 110/110 cum laude and a special mention of merit. Her final performance included various compositions by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart.

Since 2025, she has been pursuing a Second-Level Postgraduate Diploma in Music Applied to Images at the Conservatory of Rovigo, further developing her interest in film scoring, orchestration and the relationship between music and visual storytelling.

Bands and Projects

Throughout her musical journey, Giorgia has been involved in several bands and musical projects, exploring different musical environments, collaborative experiences and approaches to live performance.

Among her most significant experiences was her participation in Metiria, a band with which she performed original music and took part to music competitions. With Metiria, she had the opportunity to perform an original song on the stage of the Teatro Ariston in Sanremo, achieving th third place of the well-known competition.

Her experience in bands allowed her to explore music beyond the classical repertoire, developing an interest in collective music-making, original songwriting and the creative exchange between musicians.

Alongside her band activities, Giorgia has also developed experience as a solo pianist and accompanist, working on arrangements, transcriptions and musical performances in different contexts. Her compositional work also includes "Velvet Waves", a five-minute original score conceived for the film "Volerti meno male". The composition, written for piano, flute, clarinet, two violins, cello and vibraphone, reflects her interests in chamber textures, instrumental dialogue and music specifically conceived for visual narratives.

Inspirations

Giorgia's musical influences come from different musical worlds. Her classical background is rooted in the piano repertoire, with composers such as Mozart, Liszt, Ravel and Debussy contributing to her understanding of harmony, phrasing, musical architecture and expressive possibilities.

On the rock side, Linkin Park represents one of her most significant musical inspirations. Their distinctive combination of powerful rock arrangements, electronic elements, melodic sensitivity and emotional intensity has contributed to her appreciation of music that brings together contrasting sounds and expressive dimension.

The coexistence of classical and contemporary influences plays an important role in shaping her musical curiosity and artistic identity.

She is also fascinated by the relationship between music, neuroscience and human perception, an interest that led her to participate actively in the international symposium The Neurosciences and Music VIII at the University of Helsinki in 2024.

Style

Giorgia approaches music as a form of narrative and emotional expression.

Her classical piano training provides the foundation for an approach that combines attention to harmonic structure, melodic development and instrumental color with an openness to contemporary musical languages.

Her interests range from classical interpretation and rock music to orchestration, arrangement and composition for visual media.

She is particularly interested in the ability of music to create atmospheres, evoke images and communicate emotions, as well as in the dialogue between different instruments and sound textures.

For her, music is not simply a matter of performance, but a way of telling stories and creating connections between sound and emotion.

Other Relevant Information

Alongside her musical education, Giorgia graduated in Medicine and Surgery at the University of Catania in October 2024, achieving 110/110 cum laude.

Her multidisciplinary background reflects a long-standing interest in the relationship between science, human perception and artistic expression.

She is also interested in theater and performing arts. Between 2023 and 2024, she attended a theatre workshop ath the CUT (University Theater Center) in Catania, exploring the interpretation and performance of Greek tragedies.
